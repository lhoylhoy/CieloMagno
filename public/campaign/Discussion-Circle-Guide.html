Five questions for a better conversation.

A discussion circle guide for students, org mates, and friends. No economics degree required. No data collected. Bring one source and one question.

Before you start

Pick one line. A budget item, a news story, or a public project that affects your area.
Bring one source. A DBM page, a COA report, a news article, or a local ordinance.
Agree on one rule. Every claim gets a receipt.

The five questions

  1. Which budget line in your barangay is hardest to see?
  2. What is the difference between a published budget and a transparent budget?
  3. Who bears the cost when health funds are diverted?
  4. Why can a price cap create a shortage?
  5. What would you ask a candidate to publish before election day?

Close the circle

One check. Name one thing you can verify this week.
One ask. Name one thing you can ask a public official to publish.
One share. Share the source, not the rumor.
